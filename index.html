Designer | Thinker

International Designer

I’m a senior brand and digital designer with close to ten years of experience across brand identity, digital experiences, campaigns, UI/UX and creative direction. A lot of my work has involved taking a brand system and working out how it behaves across different touchpoints, rather than treating identity as static. I’ve worked across startups and growing businesses, often moving between concept development, hands-on design, stakeholder presentations and production.

More recently, I’ve been developing the interactive and motion side of my work further, including animated brand identities, digital experiences and interface concepts.
