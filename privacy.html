Privacy & cookies

Updated 23 October 2026

This notice explains what happens when you contact Lawani St through this website and how the cookie preference controls work.

Project inquiries

The project form asks for the services you are interested in, your name and email address, and optional company, timeline, and budget details, plus a project description. After you finish the form, the website prepares an email link and a copyable brief addressed to talktolawanistreet@gmail.com. Choosing the email link asks your email app to create a draft. The website does not send that draft or save your form answers to its own server or browser storage. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email from your email app.

If you send the email, your email provider transmits it and the receiving mailbox holds it so Lawani St can read and reply. The website does not set an automatic deletion schedule for email messages. To ask about an inquiry you sent, including a request to correct or remove it, contact talktolawanistreet@gmail.com.

Third-party services

Opening an email draft uses the email app and provider you choose. Links to services such as Calendly and embedded media may take you to third-party services that have their own privacy practices. Review those providers’ notices when using their services.

Your cookie choices

You can accept or decline optional preferences, or choose analytics and marketing preferences individually. Necessary browser storage is always enabled so the website can remember your choice.

What the preference tool stores

Your selection is stored in your browser’s local storage under lawani-cookie-consent. The saved record contains your necessary, analytics, and marketing choices, the consent version, and the time the choice was saved. This preference record is stored on your device and is not sent to Lawani St by the preference tool. You can remove it by clearing this website’s local storage in your browser.

Optional technologies

Choosing analytics or marketing records your preference. The preference tool itself does not load analytics or marketing services. If optional services are added to the website, they should respect the choices saved here. Embedded or linked third-party services may use their own technologies and are governed by their providers’ privacy notices.

Contact

For questions about this notice or information you sent to Lawani St, contact talktolawanistreet@gmail.com.
