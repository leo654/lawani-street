Cookie and browser storage notice · 10 October 2026

This notice explains how the cookie preference controls on the Lawani St website work. It focuses on cookies and similar technologies used by this website.

Your cookie choices

You can accept or decline optional preferences, or choose analytics and marketing preferences individually. Necessary browser storage is always enabled so the website can remember your choice.

What the preference tool stores

Your selection is stored in your browser’s local storage under lawani-cookie-consent. The saved record contains your necessary, analytics, and marketing choices, the consent version, and the time the choice was saved. This preference record is stored on your device and is not sent to Lawani St by the preference tool. You can remove it by clearing this website’s local storage in your browser.

Optional technologies

Choosing analytics or marketing records your preference. The preference tool itself does not load analytics or marketing services. If optional services are added to the website, they should respect the choices saved here. Embedded or linked third-party services may use their own technologies and are governed by their providers’ privacy notices.

Contact

For questions about this cookie and browser storage notice, contact accounts@lawani.st.
